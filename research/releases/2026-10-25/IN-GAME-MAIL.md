# A familiar workshop, new journeys

Date: 2026-10-25. Prepared for the in-game Mail screen; not installed. No reward assigned.

Welcome back! The workshop has a refreshed look, with original Potion Maker artwork, the game font, clearer brewing controls, and layouts adapted for phone and desktop screens.

Look for the restored quest portraits, ingredient rack, menu icons, potion-level artwork, and cauldron bubbles, splashes, and enhancement effects. Original music and action sounds are included; sound still follows your settings. The event banners are staying!

Dolbur now has playable chapters for expedition tiers 1–5 across Life, Mana, and Stamina. Completed chapters stay in the Story journal, and older trips keep their saved outcomes.

The conveyor now separates movement from ingredient emission. Its base rate runs from 1 to 20 cards per second; supported fairy bonuses can raise that rate. An empty cauldron is capped at 3 cards per second after those bonuses. Pushcats pause when the cauldron is empty or the potion cannot be enhanced further.

The Shop offers an Enhancement Pack and ruby, ticket, and gold bundles using earned currencies. The Bank has been removed; ticket and ruby bundles keep the same value of 10 tickets to 1 ruby. Gold-bundle quotes reflect your permanent progression.

Shop, Save, and Mail remain available in the menu. Existing saves remain supported. As always, export a backup before moving the game to another browser.
