# Expedition rewards

Custom prototype rewards; character specialties are original adaptations, not original-game reward tables.

Every new trip grants likeability (10 + 2 × zero-based job tier), a three-charge visitor supply bundle multiplied by the material band (tiers 1–5, 6–10, 11–15, 16–20, 21–25), and stamps (1–5 for supply errands; 2–6 for fairy searches). Supply errands also have a 20% bonus chance: Luke/Piria find 1–5 tickets by band; other visitors find one ruby. Searches instead offer a 40% targeted fairy chance, guaranteed after two misses for that fairy. Supplies, stamps and likeability remain guaranteed on a missed search.

Fairy availability combines a visitor pool and a material-tier gate. The economy configuration records the exact pools and gates. Starter fairies begin at tier 1; ingredient fairies at 2; enhancement fairies at 6; sale/protection at 10; advanced at 14; helper specialists at 18; late additions at 22; Behe at 25. The same tier must be unlocked across all three material families. Kyle has the broadest pool; other visitors specialise.

The Stamp Exchange sells one boost charge for 5 stamps, one ticket for 10 stamps (material tier 6), or one fairy copy for 12 + 2 × its unlocking tier stamps. Fairy exchanges share material gates and refuse maxed fairies. Unlocked fairy duplicates raise their level as usual. All 42 fairy entries are included. Skin access is not changed.

Rewards are frozen at dispatch. In-flight legacy trips preserve old rewards, including old three-charge supply parcels. Stamps, fairy-search misses and promised rewards are validated and saved. New trips still require a visitor currently offering/waiting for a potion order; dispatch replaces that order. No new cash currency is introduced.

Targeted checks cover visitor/tier eligibility, arrival replacement, concurrent commissions, save/load, guaranteed search progression, target selection, scaled reward snapshots, stamp accounting, exchange locks and old-trip compatibility. Prices and reward quantities are prototype balance values, not a new full economy calibration.
