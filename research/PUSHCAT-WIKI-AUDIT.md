# Pushcat wiki audit — 17 October 2026

Sources: [Pushcat](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Pushcat), [Blackcat](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Blackcat), [Upgrades](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrades), [Fairy](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Fairy), [Achievements](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Achievements), [HALAK](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/Halak_Birthday_event), [Summer part 2](https://potionmaker.fandom.com/wiki/2015_Summer_Event_Part._2).

The wiki confirms 15 Pushcat levels, one ticket per summon, 60 working seconds, empty-cauldron pausing, and faster Blackcat collection. Exact speed intervals, gold prices, base Blackcat odds and percentage stacking are not published. Existing 1000→300 ms Pushcat / 2x Blackcat rates, gold prices and 5% base odds remain explicitly custom. Eight fixed head positions are the player's requested adaptation. Normal catch-rate caps are 1/interval, not a guarantee that an ingredient is available overhead.

Upgrade purchases immediately scale each active cat's interval and remaining cooldown proportionally, preserving its frozen fairy bonus. Duration, equipped-fairy bonuses and event eligibility are fixed per summon. Current rates are displayed on the summon control and upgrade panel.

Added collectible Pushcat Fairy (duration +5→25%, speed +12→30%), Blackcat Fairy (duration −5→50%, chance +5→40 percentage points), Halloprin (material +5%, speed +10%, duration −5%; fixed because only base values are published), and Behe (helper duration/speed +35%, chance +11 points at level 10). Behe's unspecified earlier helper effects are not interpolated. Existing Magi-Run, Warri-Run, Poong-Poong, King-Poong and Hero-Poong helper bonuses remain connected. Forest/Fire/Water Fairy retain their previously supplied fixed values rather than being renamed into Blackcat Fairy.

Thief-Run ticket interval −5→10%, King-Poong −10→20%, Behe −15% at max are adapted as per-emission ticket probability divided by (1−reduction). The wiki's available-time wording is ambiguous; this is labeled as an adaptation. Material emission and settings still affect overall supply. Arch-Run / Gentle-Poong / max Behe requested-material bonuses now increase the relative weight of an accepted quest's exact ingredient; they do not change its ticket/drop kind probabilities.

HALAK's Birthday doubles the working duration of summons made during its active 15 minutes. Summer 2015 part 2 multiplies the combined Blackcat chance by five, capped at 100%. Fairy bonus then event multiplier is our explicit stacking choice. Breaks have no summon bonuses; already summoned cats retain theirs. No other unsupported historical multiplier was invented.

Summon and helper-catch statistics are saved. Documented automatic milestone gifts at 100/500 summons grant 1 ruby + 5 tickets; 2000 grants 1 ruby + 10 tickets. Missing historical totals start at zero. Other thresholds have no invented reward. Failed save refunds include currency and statistics rollback.

All four new fairies use existing local original artwork and join the current fairy reward pool (our browser game's acquisition route). Historical event-request acquisition is not recreated; the current event redemption and chest system remains the collection route. Enhancing-duration abilities remain unsupported because enhancement is instantaneous. Broad progression/ROI calibration and browser visual timing are still pending.

Helper catches now include tickets and active-event Miracle Solution. They use the same balance limits and event eligibility as manual collection; ordinary ingredient statistics exclude special drops. Currency catches trigger immediate saves and fly toward their balances. Empty/hidden pauses and overhead-only catches still apply.
