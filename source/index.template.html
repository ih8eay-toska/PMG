How to play

A handful of herbs, a little luck, and something wonderful in the cauldron.

  1. Pick a passing ingredient to start a potion.
  2. Add more ingredients or enhance to multiply its value.
  3. Sell before luck runs out and grow your shop.

The brewing loop

Ingredients, luck, and a growing shop

Catch any passing ingredient to start a potion. The conveyor contains randomized unlocked ingredients and runs even when the cauldron is empty. The first ingredient fixes the potion; later ingredients add value.

Enhance multiplies its value but risks losing the potion. Sell banks the coins. Life potions have changing multipliers and chances; stamina potions repeat the same multiplier and chance at every level.

Travelers arrive at random. Accept an order to start its one-minute preparation deadline. Deliver the requested potion before time runs out; rewards arrive immediately upon delivery. Successful orders give EXP based on potion family and material tier; higher visitor levels unlock Requests. Request costs and durations follow the wiki, and rewards include likeability plus chance-based bonuses. An expired quest gives no reward and keeps your potion. Protection is consumed only on failed enhancement, enhancement bonus adds ten percentage points to an attempt, and sell bonus doubles a sale. These charges apply automatically. Equip one collected fairy in the Fairies book; duplicates raise its level and its passive boost affects brewing, sales, ingredients, or visitor timing. Successful traveler quests also award 1 ruby for permanent upgrades in the Ruby shop. Spend 10 Pushcat tickets in the Fairy House for a random fairy; duplicates raise its level.

Material Emission speeds up the conveyor during brewing, preserving even spacing. An empty cauldron always uses the same base conveyor speed. The Potion book lists enhancement values for all 75 potions.

Keys 1–6 collect the first six visible ingredients · E enhance · S sell. Save tools exports and imports progress. The Music button plays the locally bundled theme.
Evidence and fidelity

Reconstructing the original loop

75 potion entries and their recorded numeric values come from the Potion and Materials articles. That page lists names, base prices, and starting potions; it has no individual flavor descriptions, so the Material book uses labeled fan-written interpretations. The potion figures are supplemented by the hyphenated wiki’s Potions table. Powerup behavior, quest rewards, EXP, traveler levels, and normal request costs, durations, and base reward odds use both wikis.

All 75 enhancement tables are complete using both wikis. Home MP and Small MP use the hyphenated wiki’s internally consistent values. Source disagreements remain recorded in the Potion book and research notes; these tables have not been verified against your iPhone version.

Still provisional: upgrade costs and intermediate increments (retained at your request), exact conveyor speed and emission scaling, sale rounding, enhancement timing, visitor arrival intervals, order distributions, and missing quest EXP entries (interpolated). Conveyor behavior, additive ingredient values, random arrivals, and one-minute accepted-order deadlines follow your observations. Normal-quest response text is fan-written; original portraits and documented reward bundles are used. Seven additional visitors temporarily give a guaranteed random regular fairy for a successful timed order. This shortcut replaces their original request/challenge reward behavior. Supported fairy abilities now affect gameplay. The Fairy House spends Pushcat tickets on fairy draws; full Pushcat/Blackcat event systems, costumes, and quest material-rate abilities remain future work. Requests can currently be started only after active visitors are dismissed; mid-quest cancellation is not available.

The original assets, including music, are credited to Sinsiroad and the original artists/composers. Wiki documentation is credited to its contributors. All game files and progress remain local. The Statistics screen uses the wiki achievement categories as a reference for coins collected, successful/failed enhancements, ingredients used and level-1 sales, plus local gameplay breakdowns. Counters unavailable in older saves begin at migration and are marked as tracked history.

Your shop in numbers

Statistics

Ruby upgrades

Arcane Atelier

Earn 1 ruby for every successful traveler quest. Spend rubies on permanent shop boosts.

Pushcat ticket rolls

Fairy House

Spend 10 Pushcat tickets to draw a random fairy. Duplicates raise its level up to 10; fairies already at level 10 are excluded.

Little companions

Fairy collection

Equip one collected fairy for its passive boost. Each duplicate raises its level (one level per duplicate, up to 10). Some wiki abilities need game systems this prototype does not have yet.

Visitor progression

Request board

Quest orders award EXP. Select a visitor and an unlocked request; its coin cost is paid up front. The timer continues while the page is closed.

Likeability is tracked as a total; the wikis do not specify whether points go to Pio or Tia. Quest EXP values missing from the wiki table are interpolated.
