Original-game audio supplied by the project owner

These WAVs were copied from the owner's local PotionMaker-REA/Work/audio_test folder. The source files are kept unchanged. This personal, unpublished prototype makes no separate license claim for original-game audio.

Currently embedded and used:
- Plop.wav — ingredient landing (manual/conveyor), timed to the existing 480 ms fall; max three voices.
- Pushcat Hit.wav — helper ingredient catch; one reusable player with a 120 ms cooldown to prevent a rapid barrage.
- Enhance Failed.wav — enhancement failure, including protected failures.
- Enhanced.wav — ordinary successful enhancement.
- Congratulation.wav — enhancement that unlocks a mastery achievement or Tia; replaces, rather than layers with, the ordinary success cue.
- Sell.wav — ordinary potion sale only.
- Quest.wav — accepting a visitor order.
- Quest Complete.wav — delivering a visitor order.
- Summon Pushcat.wav — successfully saved helper summon.
- UI Click.wav — menu navigation and dialog close/back controls only, at low volume.

Music:
- Main Theme.wav is now embedded as the owner-supplied original theme. The former wiki-downloaded Main_Theme.ogg was removed.

Archived but not embedded:
- Enhancing.wav is about ten seconds while the game's enhancement timer is three seconds. Playing or cutting it to fit would be misleading, so no enhancement-start sound is used.

The third-party CC0 cauldron splash remains in assets/audio/ with its original license note, but the game now uses the original Plop recording.
