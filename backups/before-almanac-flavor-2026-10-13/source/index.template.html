The brewing loop

Ingredients, luck, and a growing shop

Choose the starting ingredient for your potion from three families. You can select any unlocked tier. Once brewing begins, click ingredients as they pass along the top bar. The first ingredient fixes the potion; later ingredients add value.

Enhance multiplies its value but risks losing the potion. Sell banks the coins. Life potions have changing multipliers and chances; stamina potions repeat the same multiplier and chance at every level.

Travelers accept an enhanced potion. Their rewards arrive after one minute while you keep brewing. Protection is consumed only on failed enhancement, enhancement bonus adds ten percentage points to an attempt, and sell bonus doubles a sale. These charges apply automatically.

Material Emission increases the supply during brewing; it does not affect the starting choices. The Potion book lists all 75 wiki entries and identifies missing data.

Keys 1–6 collect the first six visible ingredients · E enhance · S sell. Save tools exports and imports progress. The Music button plays the locally bundled theme.
Evidence and fidelity

Reconstructing the original loop

75 potion entries and their recorded numeric values come from the Potion and Materials articles. Powerup behavior and normal quest reward bundles/delay come from Powerup and Quests.

69 enhancement tables are complete. Missing steps are disabled rather than filled with invented values. Source disagreements are recorded in the Potion book and research notes.

Still provisional: upgrade costs and intermediate increments, ingredient movement/supply timing, ingredient distributions, sale rounding and post-enhancement addition, immediate enhancement timing, visitor availability, and exact quest requirements. The three repeatable traveler orders use the documented rewards with provisional potion targets. Requests, fairy effects, costumes, and Pushcat are archived for future implementation.

The original assets, including music, are credited to Sinsiroad and the original artists/composers. Wiki documentation is credited to its contributors. All game files and progress remain local.
