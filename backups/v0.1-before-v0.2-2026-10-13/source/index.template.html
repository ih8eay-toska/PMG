The brewing loop

Make a little. Risk a little.

Click any ingredient on the shelf. The first one fixes the potion’s family and tier; further ingredients of any color add to its value.

Enhance multiplies the current value. Check the displayed success chance: a failed attempt destroys the potion. Sell banks the coins safely and clears the cauldron.

Buy ingredient upgrades to unlock new potions and better shelf ingredients. Ingredients are always free, so a failed brew never leaves you stuck.

Keyboard: 1–6 collect shelf slots · E enhance · S sell. Your coins, upgrades, stats, and current potion save automatically. Export a backup before moving this file or switching browsers.

This first version includes nine potions. It does not yet include travelers, quests, costumes, protection items, or offline earnings.
About this recreation

A playable first chapter

This unofficial prototype reconstructs the basic loop from public wiki documentation. It is not an exact reproduction of the installed iPhone build.

  • Materials: ingredient identities, values, and starting potion behavior.
  • Potion: first three potion tiers in each family, base prices, enhancement multipliers, and chances.
  • Upgrades: upgrade categories.
  • Pio: character illustration. The 18 ingredient/potion images are copied from their linked wiki image files.

Wiki contributors provide the documentation. Game images remain credited to Sinsiroad and the original artists; the wiki’s text license is not assumed to license every image. No game code is included.

Prototype choices: upgrade prices and increments, shelf size and refill timing, rounding at sale, and how adding ingredients interacts with existing enhancement. These need comparison with the phone. There are no in-app purchases, accounts, or network dependencies.
